# New Release

New Release is the form for publishing music: create an album, fill in its details, and upload its tracks. When you're done, the release appears in your [Catalog](./catalog.md).

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** the New Release form.

:::note New releases start private
A newly created release is **closed to every audience** until you set its access. After creating it, open the album in your [Catalog](./catalog.md) to choose public and fan-club release windows — immediate, scheduled, or fan-only.
:::

## Album details

Upload a cover image (recommended, up to 5 MB), then fill in the album fields — grouped into required and optional below.

**Required**

- **Album Title**
- **Release Year**
- **Release Type** — Single, EP, Album, Compilation, Demo, Live, or Soundtrack
- **Primary Genre**
- **Release Date**
- **Label Name** — defaults to "Independent"
- **Copyright Line (℗)** — your sound-recording copyright, e.g. "℗ 2026 Artist Name"
- **Publishing Rights (©)** — your composition copyright, e.g. "© 2026 Artist Name"

**Optional**

- **Album cover** image (up to 5 MB)
- **Secondary Genre**
- **UPC / EAN barcode** — a 12–13 digit code (validated if you enter one)
- **Language** — defaults to English
- **Explicit content** advisory (album-wide)
- **Re-release** — mark this if the content was previously published
- **Pre-order date** — must fall before the release date

## Tracks

Click the upload area to select your audio files. You can **select multiple files at once**.

- **Formats:** MP3, FLAC, or WAV — up to **100 MB each**
- Station reads each file's **title, duration, sample rate, and bit depth** automatically and shows them on the track row.
- **Drag tracks to reorder** them — track numbers follow the order you set.
- Click a track's **title** to rename it, or add optional **per-track artwork** (up to 5 MB).

Click **More** on any track to edit its metadata:

- **ISRC** code (validated if you enter one)
- **Explicit content** advisory (per track)
- **Featured artists** (comma-separated)
- **Lyrics**

## Create the release

Once your album has a title and at least one track, click **Create Release**. Station uploads your cover and tracks, then takes you to your [Catalog](./catalog.md) where the new album is waiting.

Remember: it starts **closed** until you set its access.
