# Welcome to Station

Run your own decentralized music server and share your music directly with listeners.

## What is Station?

Station is a decentralized music streaming platform that puts **you** in control. Instead of uploading to a central platform, you run your own server and stream music directly to listeners through a peer-to-peer network.

**No middlemen. No platform risk. Just you and your fans.**

## Why Station?

- **You own it** — Your server, your music, your rules
- **Can't be shut down** — No company can deplatform you or change the terms
- **Direct connection** — Get paid directly by your supporters
- **Always available** — As long as your server runs, your music is accessible

:::tip Testnet is live

Station is up and running on **testnet** — a great way to try it out and see if running your own server is right for you. Everything works just like it will on mainnet, on a separate network built for testing. (Mainnet launches soon — we'll update these docs the moment it does.)

:::

## How setup works

Getting a Station server running takes three steps:

1. **Get ready** — grab a cheap server (or use a machine at home) and a domain name. See [What You Need](./what-you-need.md).
2. **Run one command** — the installer sets up everything for you: Docker, HTTPS, and your Station node. See [Install Station](../deployment/install.md).
3. **Log in and upload** — the installer gives you a PIN to create your dashboard login, then you're ready to add music.

The whole thing takes about 10 minutes.
