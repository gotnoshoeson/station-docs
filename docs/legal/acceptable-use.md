# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** `2026-11-27` · **Effective:** 27 November 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy is part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) between you and Colour Labs LLC ("Colour Labs", "we", "us"). Words defined in the Terms mean the same here.

## 1. What this policy is

Station is software that artists run on their own servers, and the QNCH Network is the network those servers form. Colour Labs doesn't host Content, and the Services have no feature that lets us review, remove, block or de-list Content or a name.

So this policy is a set of **binding rules for everyone who uses the Services**: artists, operators and listeners. Breaking them is a breach of the Terms, with the consequences in § 6 and the indemnity in § 13 of the Terms. It is **not a promise that we will monitor or police the network**. Each artist and operator is responsible for what is published from their own server.

## 2. What you must not do

You must not use the Station software, the listener app, our boot nodes, the contracts or the QNCH Network to publish, distribute, sell, request or help with any of the following.

### 2.1 Content

- **Infringement.** Content you don't have the rights to publish, or that infringes anyone's copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity or other rights. See the [Copyright Policy](/legal/copyright).
- **Child sexual abuse material.** This is absolutely prohibited (§ 4).
- **Other illegal content**, including content that promotes or helps with terrorism, violent crime, human trafficking, or the exploitation of anyone.
- **Non-consensual intimate images**, and threats to share them.

### 2.2 Conduct

- **Impersonation and deception.** Pretending to be another artist or person; registering a name to mislead listeners about who you are; or misrepresenting what a Fan Club contribution or Station Shop sale is. That includes describing a contribution as an investment, as giving a share of revenue or profit, or as a charitable or tax-deductible donation.
- **Fraud and scams.** Phishing; fake sites, apps or contracts presented as Station's or an artist's; schemes to take people's funds, keys or recovery phrases.
- **Illegal finance.** Using the Services to launder money, finance terrorism, or get around sanctions.
- **Attacks on the network or on people's servers.** Distributing malware; attacking, overloading or disrupting Station servers, our boot nodes, the listener app or the network; flooding the network with name claims or requests; or trying to take over names that belong to others.
- **Getting around access controls.** Bypassing the access an artist has set on their Content, or helping others to.
- **Harvesting and surveillance.** Scraping, collecting or de-anonymizing information about other people using the network.
- **Harassment.** Threats, targeted harassment, or publishing someone's private information without their consent.

These lists are examples. They don't cover everything that is unlawful or harmful.

## 3. Artists' and operators' responsibilities

If you run a Station server, you are responsible for:

- everything published from it;
- following the laws that apply to you and your Content, wherever you and your listeners are;
- responding to complaints from rights holders and others, directly or through your hosting provider; and
- the configuration and security of your server.

Colour Labs can't remove Content from your server. That responsibility is yours alone.

## 4. Child sexual abuse material

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is absolutely prohibited on the Services and on the QNCH Network. If Colour Labs becomes aware of apparent CSAM, we will report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and we will cooperate with law enforcement and with valid legal process.

If you come across CSAM anywhere, report it to NCMEC's CyberTipline at [report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org) or 1-800-843-5678, and to your local law enforcement. Don't download, copy or share it, even to report it.

## 5. Reporting a problem

- **Copyright:** follow the [Copyright Policy](/legal/copyright).
- **Anything else unlawful or abusive:** report it to the artist running the server and to that server's hosting provider, and, where it may be a crime, to law enforcement. The [Copyright Policy](/legal/copyright) explains how to find out which server and hosting provider are involved.
- **You can also tell us** at colourlabs@proton.me. We'll respond as far as we can and as the law requires, but a report to us can't remove Content from an artist's server.

## 6. What happens if you break this policy

We can't remove Content from a server or remove anyone from the network. What we can do is limited to the services we run ourselves: we may stop providing any of them to someone who breaks this policy (§ 15 of the Terms), and we cooperate with law enforcement and valid legal process. Breaking this policy may also make you liable to others and to us, including under the indemnity in § 13 of the Terms.
